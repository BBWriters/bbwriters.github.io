04 / Good questions

A few things you might ask.

What kinds of projects can I ask about?

Ghostwriting, editing and proofreading, eBook writing, and content for businesses and digital publishers.

Can I get help if I only have an outline?

Yes. Share your outline and describe what you would like your finished project to become.

Can I request editing instead of new writing?

Yes. Tell us the type and approximate length of your draft.

How are pricing and timelines decided?

They depend on the scope, length, complexity and timing of the project. Reach out to discuss your needs.
